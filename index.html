Services

What we do

We provide marketing services to businesses that sell online. We do not sell products ourselves.

Google Ads Management

We plan, set up and manage Search and Shopping campaigns on Google Ads, and send clients a regular report on spend and results.

Google Merchant Center Setup

We set up Google Merchant Center accounts and help stores meet Google's Shopping guidelines, including clear store policies and accurate product information.

Product Feed Management

We organize and maintain product feeds (titles, prices, images and categories) so products are listed correctly on Google Shopping.
