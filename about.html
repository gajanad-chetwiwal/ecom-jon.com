About Us

A small, founder-led Google specialist.

ECOMJON LLC is based in McCall, Idaho. We help eCommerce stores get approved on Google Merchant Center and grow with Google Ads. We're small on purpose — the person who audits your account is the one who fixes it.

JI

Jon Sigurd Iverson

Founder, ECOMJON LLC

Jon started Ecom Jon after seeing the same problem again and again: good stores with good products, blocked from Google Shopping by suspensions and disapprovals nobody could explain — or running Google Ads that looked fine on the dashboard but lost money in the bank account.

Ecom Jon is built to fix both. First we get your store compliant and approved. Then we run Google Ads focused on real profit, with plain-English weekly reports and no long-term contracts.

We're a new agency, and we say so. Instead of borrowed logos and made-up numbers, we offer a free audit with written findings you keep, month-to-month terms, and the founder working directly on your account.
